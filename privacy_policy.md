# Privacy Policy — IELTS Vocab

Last updated: 20.04.2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data the app handles and how.

## Summary
- General audience app.
- Most features work without sign-in.
- If you sign in, we create a basic account and may sync your learning data across your devices.
- Usage analytics are collected to improve the app.
- Ads are delivered by Google Mobile Ads. Whether ads are personalized depends on your App Tracking Transparency (ATT) choice.
- Subscriptions are processed by Apple; we receive purchase status only.

## Data We Handle

**Account information (only if you sign in)**
- Email address (from Sign in with Apple; may be a private relay address if you choose)
- A unique account identifier
- Username you set

**Learning data**
- Progress, completed sets, saved words, preferences, daily goal configuration
- Stored on your device and, if signed in, synced to our secure backend so you can continue across devices

**Subscription data**
- Subscription status (active/expired), plan, purchase history provided by the App Store
- We do not receive payment card details

**Usage analytics (product improvement)**
- Anonymous usage events: screens viewed, features used, session duration, crashes/errors
- A randomly generated analytics identifier; if you are signed in, events are associated with your account identifier so we can understand retention and feature adoption
- Approximate country (derived from IP at the time of the request; we do not store precise location)
- Device type, operating system version, app version, language

**Advertising data (free tier users)**
- See the Advertising section below

## App Tracking Transparency (iOS)
On first launch, iOS will ask whether you allow the app to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites.

- **If you allow:** the advertising identifier (IDFA) may be shared with our ad provider to deliver personalized ads.
- **If you don't allow:** ads are still shown but are non-personalized; IDFA is not accessed.

Your choice does not affect your ability to use the app. You can change this at any time in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking**.

## Advertising
We use Google Mobile Ads to display ads to free-tier users. Google may collect device-related information to deliver, frequency-cap, and measure ads. Whether Google receives the advertising identifier (IDFA) depends on your ATT choice above.

For details on Google's data practices, see Google's privacy policy. You can reset your advertising identifier and limit ad tracking in device Settings.

## Third-Party Service Providers
We rely on trusted service providers to operate the app. These providers process data on our behalf under contractual confidentiality and data protection obligations:

- **Authentication and backend hosting** — to authenticate sign-in, store your account, sync learning data, and serve content
- **Product analytics** — to collect anonymous usage events that help us understand how the app is used and improve it
- **Subscription management** — to verify App Store purchases and maintain subscription status across your devices
- **Advertising** — Google Mobile Ads, as described above

These providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States. We select providers that offer appropriate safeguards for international data transfers.

We do not sell your personal data.

## Local Storage
The app stores data on your device for functionality and offline use (progress, saved words, cached content, preferences). Removing the app or clearing its data deletes this local data.

## Cloud Sync (optional)
If you sign in, we link your learning data to your account so you can restore it across devices. We store only the minimum information necessary to provide this functionality.

## Data Retention and Deletion
- Account and synced data are retained while your account exists.
- Anonymous analytics may be retained in aggregated form for up to 24 months.
- Local device data remains on your device; uninstalling the app removes it.
- To request deletion of your account data, contact: cleevao@gmail.com.
- If you purchased a subscription through Apple, cancel it in the App Store. Account deletion does not cancel billing.

## Your Rights and Choices
- **Access, correction, deletion:** Contact us to exercise these rights over your account data.
- **Analytics opt-out:** Contact us at cleevao@gmail.com to opt out of analytics collection.
- **Advertising:** Manage personalization via the ATT prompt and in device privacy settings.
- **Local data:** Control via device settings or by uninstalling the app.

Depending on your jurisdiction (EU/EEA, UK, California, etc.), you may have additional rights including data portability and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

## Children
This app is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your region). If you believe a child has provided data to us, please contact us and we will delete it.

## Security
We use industry-standard security measures, including encryption in transit (HTTPS) and at rest for sensitive data, access controls, and regular security reviews. No system is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

## Legal Basis (EU/EEA/UK)
- **Account and learning data:** performance of our agreement with you.
- **Analytics:** our legitimate interest in improving the app, balanced against your rights; you may opt out.
- **Personalized ads:** your consent, given through the ATT prompt.
- **Non-personalized ads:** our legitimate interest in providing a free tier supported by advertising.
- **Legal compliance:** where processing is required to meet a legal obligation.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy as the app or laws change. The "Last updated" date reflects the latest version. Material changes will be highlighted in-app or by other reasonable means.
